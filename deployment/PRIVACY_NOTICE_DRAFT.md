# GeoSustain privacy notice — owner review required

This is a working notice for the project owner to complete and approve before publication. Supply the real controller, support contact, retention periods, hosting regions, provider agreements and dataset/map rights. A software test does not establish legal compliance.

GeoSustain stores the account information you supply, password hashes, verified identity/phone linkage, profile photos, farm names/boundaries and coordinates, analyses, saved reports and review decisions. It uses account roles to limit access. Agricultural planning analysts can see submissions within their authorized queue; administrators manage access and reference configuration.

Environmental and mapping services receive the coordinates or tile requests needed for their features. Depending on configured features, Google Earth Engine, weather providers and map-tile hosts process those requests. Firebase/Google process Google sign-in, phone verification and push registration; the configured mail provider processes verification/recovery mail. Exact provider/account configuration and applicable terms must be confirmed by the owner before launch.

Android tokens and offline farm workspaces use Android Keystore-backed encryption. Browser sessions/workspaces use origin storage and require appropriate device/browser and application protections. Pending farms can remain on the device for the same account and sync when connected. Do not describe browser origin storage as secure hardware encryption.

Deleting an account removes its owned farm boundaries/coordinates, analyses, photos, reports, challenges, jobs and notification subscriptions. Audit decisions retain pseudonymous references rather than the deleted profile identity. Owner-approved backups/export retention and their deletion schedule still need to be specified; deleting server data does not automatically recall files a user has exported.

Notification permission is optional at device/browser level. In-app activity and push-provider acceptance are distinct; disabling a subscription removes its pending deliveries. SMS/email codes are limited and expire. Recovery changes the password version so old app sessions are refused.

Crop/environment comparisons are decision support. Historical locality soil records, modelled environmental observations, missing factors and preliminary results have different limits. The app does not establish cadastral ownership, certify a soil test or guarantee yield. See the source and missing-data information shown with each analysis.
