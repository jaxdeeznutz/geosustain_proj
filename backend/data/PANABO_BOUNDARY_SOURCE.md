# Panabo City reference boundary

`panabo_boundary.geojson` extracts the single **City of Panabo** municipality from geoBoundaries gbOpen PHL ADM3, represented year **2020**, revision **9469f09**. Simplified geometry is retained unchanged; attribution and screening metadata were added to its properties.

Source institutions: **National Mapping and Resource Information Authority (NAMRIA), Philippines Statistics Authority (PSA), OCHA Philippines**, distributed through geoBoundaries.

- [geoBoundaries metadata API](https://www.geoboundaries.org/api/current/gbOpen/PHL/ADM3/)
- [Pinned source geometry](https://github.com/wmgeolab/geoBoundaries/blob/9469f09/releaseData/gbOpen/PHL/ADM3/geoBoundaries-PHL-ADM3_simplified.geojson)
- [Original source dataset](https://data.humdata.org/dataset/philippines-administrative-levels-0-to-3)
- Licence: [CC BY 3.0 IGO](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/igo/)

This is an administrative reference for preliminary screening, not a cadastral survey, land title, exact farm boundary or claim that every farm has a CAGRO sample. Display its source/year and retain uncertainty near administrative edges. Soil association is checked separately and missing CAGRO NPK stays unavailable.
